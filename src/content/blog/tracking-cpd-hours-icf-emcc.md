Renewal season brings out a particular kind of panic in coaches. The frantic search through old emails for workshop confirmations. The spreadsheet that was supposed to track everything but stopped getting updated in March. The quiet dread of realising you might be short on hours.

It does not have to be like this.

## How many hours do you actually need?

The requirements differ between ICF and EMCC, and they change depending on your credential level. Here is the short version.

**ICF (International Coaching Federation):**
- ACC renewal: 40 hours of continuing education, including at least 24 hours of Coach Specific Education and 10 hours of Core Competency learning
- PCC renewal: 40 hours, same breakdown
- MCC renewal: 40 hours, same breakdown
- Renewal cycle: every three years

**EMCC (European Mentoring and Coaching Council):**
- Requirements vary by level (Foundation, Practitioner, Senior Practitioner, Master Practitioner)
- Typically 30 hours of CPD over two years at Practitioner level, scaling up at higher levels
- EMCC also expects evidence of reflective practice, not just hours logged

Check the current requirements directly with your accrediting body. They update these periodically, and the details matter when you are close to the deadline.

## What actually counts as CPD?

This is where coaches often trip up. CPD is broader than most people think, but it is not unlimited.

**Almost always counts:**
- Accredited coach training programmes and workshops
- Supervision sessions (individual or group)
- Webinars and online courses on coaching skills or competencies
- Coaching conferences and events
- Mentor coaching (received, not delivered)
- Formal study in related fields like psychology, leadership, or organisational development

**Often counts, but check the specifics:**
- Peer coaching circles and practice groups
- Reading and self-study (ICF has specific rules about how many self-study hours you can claim)
- Writing articles or research on coaching topics
- Delivering training on coaching (some bodies cap this)
- Pro bono coaching hours (these sometimes count toward practice hours rather than CPD)

**Probably does not count:**
- General business development courses (marketing, sales, accounting)
- Personal therapy or counselling you receive
- Informal conversations with other coaches over coffee

The distinction that matters most: CPD should develop your coaching capability. If an activity makes you a better coach, it probably counts. If it makes you a better business owner but does not touch your coaching practice, it probably does not.

## Common mistakes at renewal time

Having spoken to hundreds of coaches about their CPD tracking, the same problems come up again and again.

**Waiting until the last six months.** If your cycle is three years long, you have time. But "having time" and "using that time well" are different things. Coaches who leave it late end up cramming workshops into the final months rather than choosing activities that genuinely interest them.

**Not keeping evidence.** Logging hours is not enough. You need certificates of completion, confirmation emails, or records from your training provider. ICF in particular can audit your renewal application. If you cannot prove you did the hours, they do not count.

**Confusing coaching hours with CPD hours.** Your client coaching hours demonstrate that you are practising. Your CPD hours demonstrate that you are developing. They are tracked separately for good reason.

**Double-counting poorly.** Some activities contribute to multiple requirements, but you need to understand the rules. A mentor coaching session might count toward both your coaching hours and your CPD, depending on the credential and how it is structured.

## How to keep an ongoing log

The coaches who never panic at renewal time all do roughly the same thing: they log CPD as they go, not retrospectively.

Here is a simple system that works:

1. **Pick one place to track everything.** A spreadsheet is fine. A coaching platform with built-in CPD tracking is better. What matters is that you use it consistently.

2. **Log within 48 hours.** After a workshop, supervision session, or webinar, spend two minutes recording it. Date, activity, provider, hours, and which competency area it relates to. If you wait a month, you will forget the details.

3. **Save your evidence immediately.** Download the certificate. Screenshot the confirmation email. Attach it to your log entry. Future you will be grateful.

4. **Review quarterly.** Spend fifteen minutes every three months looking at your log. Are you on track? Are there competency areas you have not covered? Adjust your plans accordingly.

5. **Connect CPD to your development goals.** The best CPD is not random. Pick a theme for the year. Maybe you want to deepen your systemic coaching skills, or learn more about neuroscience, or get better at working with senior executives. Intentional CPD is more useful and more motivating than box-ticking.

## Reflective practice matters too

EMCC places particular emphasis on reflective practice, and frankly, ICF is moving in that direction as well. Simply accumulating hours is not the point. Can you articulate what you learned and how it changed your practice?

Keep brief reflective notes alongside your CPD log. Two or three sentences per activity is plenty. What did you take away? What will you do differently? This is not busywork. Coaches who reflect on their development genuinely develop faster than those who just attend things and move on.

## Start now, not at renewal time

If you are reading this and your next renewal is more than six months away, you are in a great position. Set up your tracking system today. It takes ten minutes. Then maintain it as you go.

If your renewal is next month and you are underprepared, do not panic. Look at what you have done in the past year that you have not logged yet. Supervision sessions, peer groups, conferences, self-study. You may have more hours than you think. Gather the evidence, log it properly, and fill any genuine gaps with targeted activities.

Either way, make this the last renewal cycle where CPD tracking feels stressful. It really does not need to be.
